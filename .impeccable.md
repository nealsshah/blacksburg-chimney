# Design Context — Blacksburg Chimney Services

## Target Audience
Homeowners in rural Virginia (New River Valley area). Not upscale — practical, down-to-earth people who value reliability and experience.

## Brand Personality
**Trusted & Rugged.** 48+ years in business, 3 CSIA certified pros. Blue-collar craftsmanship pride. These are the guys you call because your neighbor trusts them.

## Desired Feeling
"I trust these guys" — not premium or flashy. Good choices, established, dependable. The kind of business that shows up on time and does the job right.

## Visual Direction
Warm, earthy, high-contrast. Craftsman aesthetic. Confident typography. Strong use of real photography. No logo currently — the name and reputation carry the brand.

## Constraints
- Next.js 14 + Tailwind CSS + Framer Motion
- Must emphasize: 3 CSIA Certified Professionals, BBB Accredited, 48+ years experience
- Client likes hearthsidehelpers.com style (big hero image, central contact info)
- No existing logo or strict brand guidelines
